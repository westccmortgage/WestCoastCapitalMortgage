FLORIDA CONDO FINANCING

Finance the buyer and review the building.

Florida condo financing can depend as much on the condominium project as on the borrower. We help buyers, homeowners, investors, and international purchasers review the likely financing path before they commit.

WHY CONDOS ARE DIFFERENT

The project can change the loan options.

A borrower may qualify financially while the condominium project does not fit a standard agency program. That is why early project review matters in Florida, especially for older buildings, high-rise communities, investor-heavy projects, condotels, and buildings with complex association issues.

PROJECT REVIEW

Warrantable condos

Projects that meet applicable agency or lender standards may be eligible for conventional, FHA, VA, or other standard financing depending on the borrower and property.

ALTERNATIVE PATH

Non-warrantable condos

When a project falls outside standard agency eligibility, portfolio or Non-QM programs may provide another path. Terms and project requirements vary by lender.

FLORIDA-SPECIFIC REVIEW

Insurance, reserves & association documents

Florida condo underwriting can require close review of project insurance, association financials, reserves, litigation, inspections, ownership concentration, and other building-level information.

COMMON PROJECT ISSUES

Why a condo may need a specialty financing path.

Association litigation

Pending or significant litigation can affect project eligibility depending on the nature and financial impact of the dispute.

Insurance limitations

Master-policy coverage and deductibles can affect whether a project fits a lender's requirements.

Reserve or budget concerns

Association budgets, reserves, special assessments, and deferred maintenance may become part of project review.

Investor concentration

A high percentage of non-owner-occupied units or concentrated ownership can affect standard project eligibility.

Condotel or short-term rental use

Hotel-style operations, rental desks, transient occupancy, or other condotel characteristics often require specialized programs.

New or developer-controlled projects

New construction, unsold units, or ongoing developer control may require different project documentation and lender review.

FAQ

Florida condo financing questions.

What is a non-warrantable condo?

A condo project that does not meet the eligibility standards required for standard agency financing. The issue is often project-level rather than borrower-level.

Can a non-warrantable condo still be financed?

Potentially. Portfolio and Non-QM lenders may review projects that fall outside standard agency guidelines. Availability and terms vary.

Can investors use DSCR for a Florida condo?

Some DSCR programs permit eligible condominium investment properties. Project type, rental use, HOA characteristics, short-term rental rules, and lender guidelines all matter.

Can foreign nationals finance Florida condos?

Some foreign-national and Non-QM programs may finance eligible Florida condos for qualified international buyers, subject to program and property review.
