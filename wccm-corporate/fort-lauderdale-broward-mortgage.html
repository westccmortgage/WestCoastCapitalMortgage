2026 loan amount guide

When is a Broward County mortgage jumbo?

The official 2026 FHFA one-unit conforming loan limit for Broward County, Florida, is $832,750. A loan above this amount exceeds the county's one-unit conforming limit and generally requires a jumbo or another non-conforming financing path. A loan at or below the limit must still meet applicable program and underwriting requirements.

This is a loan amount limit, not a purchase-price limit. For example, a $1,000,000 purchase with a $200,000 down payment produces an $800,000 loan before any financed costs. That amount is below $832,750; it does not establish eligibility or approval.

Source: FHFA's official 2026 county loan-limit table, Broward County, Florida (FIPS 12011). Reviewed October 2, 2026. The figure above applies to one-unit properties; two- to four-unit limits differ.

Questions before your Broward mortgage review

Does a $1 million home always require a jumbo loan?
No. The loan amount after the down payment determines whether it exceeds Broward County's $832,750 one-unit conforming limit for 2026. Property and borrower eligibility must also be reviewed.
Can I use bank statements for a Fort Lauderdale condo?
Some programs may consider alternative income documentation, but both the borrower and condo project must satisfy the selected lender's requirements. Bank statements do not waive project review.
What should I provide if my loan was rejected?
Start with the stated reason, loan amount, property type, occupancy, income source and deadline. A mortgage professional can then explain what documentation is needed for a meaningful second review.
