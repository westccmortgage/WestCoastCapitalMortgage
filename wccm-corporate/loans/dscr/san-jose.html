Overview

What a DSCR loan means in San Jose

DSCR stands for Debt-Service Coverage Ratio. A DSCR loan qualifies a San Jose investment property based on whether its rental income covers the mortgage payment, rather than on your personal income. A DSCR of 1.0 means rent equals the payment; higher ratios indicate stronger cash flow.

DSCR loans are non-conforming investor loans, so they are not capped by the conforming limit. Still, the 2026 one-unit conforming limit in Santa Clara County is $1,249,125 (per FHFA/HUD 2026 loan limits), and the typical San Jose home value is approximately $1.41M as of June 2026 (Zillow ZHVI) — useful benchmarks when you size a purchase.

Short-term rental rules

Short-term rental rules in San Jose (as of August 2026)

San Jose permits short-term rentals only in the host's primary residence (occupied at least 60 consecutive days a year). Hosted stays are uncapped; un-hosted stays are limited to 180 days per year (Municipal Code §20.80). A pure investor STR is not permitted — DSCR underwriting in San Jose should assume long-term rent, where tech-employment demand keeps the market deep.

FAQ

DSCR Loans in San Jose — common questions

Do I need to verify my income for a DSCR loan in San Jose?
No. A DSCR loan qualifies the San Jose property on whether its rental income covers the mortgage payment, rather than on your personal income documentation. A DSCR of 1.0 means rent equals the payment.
How does the 2026 loan limit affect a DSCR loan in San Jose?
San Jose is in Santa Clara County, where the 2026 one-unit conforming limit is $1,249,125 (per FHFA/HUD 2026 loan limits). DSCR loans are non-conforming investor loans, so they are not capped by that limit — but it is a useful local benchmark, since the typical San Jose home value is approximately $1.41M as of June 2026 (Zillow ZHVI).
What down payment do DSCR investors need in San Jose?
Investor programs generally look for a larger down payment than owner-occupied financing, along with reserves and a solid credit profile. The precise requirement moves with the coverage ratio the property achieves.
Can a DSCR investment property run as a short-term rental in San Jose?
No — San Jose's rules (Municipal Code §20.80) allow short-term rentals only in a host's primary residence, with un-hosted stays capped at 180 days a year. Underwrite San Jose DSCR loans on long-term market rent.
