Overview

What a DSCR loan means in San Francisco

DSCR stands for Debt-Service Coverage Ratio. A DSCR loan qualifies a San Francisco investment property based on whether its rental income covers the mortgage payment, rather than on your personal income. A DSCR of 1.0 means rent equals the payment; higher ratios indicate stronger cash flow.

DSCR loans are non-conforming investor loans, so they are not capped by the conforming limit. Still, the 2026 one-unit conforming limit in San Francisco County is $1,249,125 (per FHFA/HUD 2026 loan limits), and the typical San Francisco home value is approximately $1.4M as of June 2026 (Zillow ZHVI) — useful benchmarks when you size a purchase.

The typical San Francisco home value is approximately $1.4M as of June 2026 (Zillow ZHVI). As a dense, high-rent global tech hub, San Francisco offers deep tenant demand, giving DSCR investors a strong income basis even as elevated purchase prices keep coverage ratios in focus.

Short-term rental rules

Short-term rental rules in San Francisco (as of August 2026)

San Francisco allows short-term rentals only in a registered host's primary residence (occupied at least 275 nights a year), with un-hosted stays capped at 90 days per year and dual registration (Office of Short-Term Rentals + Treasurer). An investor property cannot legally run as a short-term rental — DSCR deals in SF are long-term rental deals.

FAQ

DSCR Loans in San Francisco — common questions

Do I need to verify my income for a DSCR loan in San Francisco?
No. A DSCR loan qualifies the San Francisco property on whether its rental income covers the mortgage payment, rather than on your personal income documentation. A DSCR of 1.0 means rent equals the payment.
How does the 2026 loan limit affect a DSCR loan in San Francisco?
San Francisco is in San Francisco County, where the 2026 one-unit conforming limit is $1,249,125 (per FHFA/HUD 2026 loan limits). DSCR loans are non-conforming investor loans, so they are not capped by that limit — but it is a useful local benchmark, since the typical San Francisco home value is approximately $1.4M as of June 2026 (Zillow ZHVI).
What down payment do DSCR investors need in San Francisco?
Investor programs generally look for a larger down payment than owner-occupied financing, along with reserves and a solid credit profile. The precise requirement moves with the coverage ratio the property achieves.
Is Airbnb income usable for a DSCR loan in San Francisco?
Not on an investment property. SF limits short-term rentals to a registered host's primary residence with a 90-day un-hosted cap. DSCR qualification in San Francisco rests on long-term market rent.
