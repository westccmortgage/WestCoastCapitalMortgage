What a jumbo loan means in Beverly Hills

A jumbo loan exceeds the conforming limit set by the Federal Housing Finance Agency. Because Beverly Hills is in Los Angeles County — a high-cost area with a 2026 one-unit conforming limit of $1,249,125 (per FHFA/HUD 2026 loan limits) — a jumbo loan in Beverly Hills is any one-unit loan above $1,249,125.

For strong borrowers, jumbo pricing is often very competitive with conforming loans, and in some cases prices close to a comparable conforming loan. What moves the rate is credit, down payment, reserves, and the specific program — not the jumbo label itself.

Beyond the mortgage

Transfer taxes and property taxes in Beverly Hills

Beverly Hills has no city transfer tax — transfers pay only the LA County rate of $1.10 per $1,000 (0.11%%). Because Beverly Hills is its own incorporated city, it is also outside Measure ULA: the 4–5.5%% City-of-LA mansion tax stops at the city line. On an $8M sale, the transfer-tax difference between Beverly Hills ($8,800) and the city of Los Angeles (roughly $364,800 including ULA at 2026 thresholds) is material enough to shape where high-end buyers and sellers transact.

California property tax follows Proposition 13: a 1% base levy on assessed value, plus voter-approved local additions that vary by tax rate area. When you buy, the assessed value resets to your purchase price, and one or two supplemental tax bills arrive after closing to cover the gap between the seller's old assessed value and yours — a real cash-flow item in your first year that buyers in Beverly Hills often don't budget for.

FAQ

Jumbo Loans in Beverly Hills — common questions

What counts as a jumbo loan in Beverly Hills?
Beverly Hills is in Los Angeles County, a high-cost area, where the 2026 one-unit conforming limit is $1,249,125 (per FHFA/HUD 2026 loan limits). A jumbo loan in Beverly Hills is any one-unit loan amount above $1,249,125.
How much are homes in Beverly Hills?
The typical Beverly Hills home value is approximately $3.69M as of June 2026 (Zillow ZHVI). Whether a specific purchase needs jumbo financing depends on the price and your down payment: the loan amount, not the purchase price, is what has to clear $1,249,125.
How much down payment do jumbo borrowers usually need in Beverly Hills?
Jumbo programs commonly look for a larger down payment — often 10 to 20 percent or more — along with strong credit and cash reserves. The exact figure depends on the loan amount, property, and your overall profile.
Can I use a jumbo loan for a second home in Beverly Hills?
Yes. Jumbo financing is available for primary residences, second homes, and many investment properties in Beverly Hills, with terms that vary by occupancy and program.
Does the LA mansion tax apply in Beverly Hills?
No. Measure ULA applies only inside the city of Los Angeles. Beverly Hills is a separate incorporated city with no city transfer tax at all — transfers pay only the $1.10 per $1,000 county rate. On multi-million-dollar sales that difference runs into the hundreds of thousands.
Is any Beverly Hills purchase realistically conforming?
Rarely. Zillow's June 2026 typical value for Beverly Hills is about $3.69M, and even the least expensive tracked neighborhoods sit near $1.8M against the $1,249,125 conforming limit. Nearly every standard purchase here is jumbo financing.
