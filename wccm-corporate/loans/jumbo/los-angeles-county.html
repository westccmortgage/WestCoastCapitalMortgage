Overview

What counts as a jumbo loan in Los Angeles County

Los Angeles County is a designated high-cost area, so its 2026 one-unit conforming loan limit is $1,249,125 (per FHFA/HUD 2026 loan limits) rather than the $832,750 national baseline. Any one-unit loan above $1,249,125 in Los Angeles County is a jumbo loan.

Values across the county run from roughly $470K in Lancaster to about $3.69M in Beverly Hills, so whether a purchase needs jumbo financing varies widely by city — the table below shows where each one sits against the $1,249,125 limit.

FAQ

Jumbo loans in Los Angeles County — common questions

What is the 2026 jumbo loan limit in Los Angeles County?
In Los Angeles County the 2026 one-unit conforming limit is $1,249,125 (per FHFA/HUD 2026 loan limits). A jumbo loan is any one-unit loan amount above $1,249,125. The county carries the high-cost limit rather than the $832,750 national baseline.
Which Los Angeles cities usually require a jumbo loan?
It varies by city. In Beverly Hills, where typical values run around $3.69M, jumbo financing applies to most standard purchases. In Lancaster, closer to $470K, it comes into play mainly on higher-end homes. The table on this page marks where each of the 40 cities we cover falls against the $1,249,125 limit.
How much down payment do jumbo borrowers need in Los Angeles?
Jumbo programs commonly look for 10 to 20 percent or more, along with a credit score generally at or above 700 and meaningful cash reserves. The exact figure depends on the loan amount, the property, and your overall profile.
Do jumbo loans cost more than conforming loans in Los Angeles?
Not necessarily. For strong borrowers jumbo pricing is often very competitive with conforming loans, and in some markets it prices close to or below a comparable conforming loan. Pricing depends on credit, down payment, reserves, and the specific program.
