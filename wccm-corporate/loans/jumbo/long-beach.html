What a jumbo loan means in Long Beach

A jumbo loan exceeds the conforming limit set by the Federal Housing Finance Agency. Because Long Beach is in Los Angeles County — a high-cost area with a 2026 one-unit conforming limit of $1,249,125 (per FHFA/HUD 2026 loan limits) — a jumbo loan in Long Beach is any one-unit loan above $1,249,125.

For strong borrowers, jumbo pricing is often very competitive with conforming loans, and in some cases prices close to a comparable conforming loan. What moves the rate is credit, down payment, reserves, and the specific program — not the jumbo label itself.

Jumbo Loans and the Long Beach market

The typical Long Beach home value is approximately $860K as of June 2026 (Zillow ZHVI). Most standard Long Beach purchases fall below the 2026 conforming ceiling, so jumbo loans here typically come into play for higher-end coastal and waterfront homes rather than the typical single-family purchase.

Across Los Angeles County, the 2026 one-unit conforming loan limit is $1,249,125 (per FHFA/HUD 2026 loan limits), set above the $832,750 national baseline because Los Angeles County is a designated high-cost area. We can walk you through exactly how that limit applies to your Long Beach scenario.

Beyond the mortgage

Transfer taxes and property taxes in Long Beach

Long Beach has no city transfer tax — transfers pay only the LA County $1.10 per $1,000, and the city is outside Measure ULA. Combined with the lowest typical values among our flagship LA-area markets, entry costs here are comparatively light.

California property tax follows Proposition 13: a 1% base levy on assessed value, plus voter-approved local additions that vary by tax rate area. When you buy, the assessed value resets to your purchase price, and one or two supplemental tax bills arrive after closing to cover the gap between the seller's old assessed value and yours — a real cash-flow item in your first year that buyers in Long Beach often don't budget for.

FAQ

Jumbo Loans in Long Beach — common questions

What counts as a jumbo loan in Long Beach?
Long Beach is in Los Angeles County, a high-cost area, where the 2026 one-unit conforming limit is $1,249,125 (per FHFA/HUD 2026 loan limits). A jumbo loan in Long Beach is any one-unit loan amount above $1,249,125.
How much are homes in Long Beach?
The typical Long Beach home value is approximately $860K as of June 2026 (Zillow ZHVI). Whether a specific purchase needs jumbo financing depends on the price and your down payment: the loan amount, not the purchase price, is what has to clear $1,249,125.
How much down payment do jumbo borrowers usually need in Long Beach?
Jumbo programs commonly look for a larger down payment — often 10 to 20 percent or more — along with strong credit and cash reserves. The exact figure depends on the loan amount, property, and your overall profile.
Can I use a jumbo loan for a second home in Long Beach?
Yes. Jumbo financing is available for primary residences, second homes, and many investment properties in Long Beach, with terms that vary by occupancy and program.
Is jumbo financing common in Long Beach?
Less than in most coastal LA markets. The typical Long Beach value (Zillow, June 2026) sits well under the $1,249,125 conforming limit, and most tracked neighborhoods price in the $600Ks-$900Ks.
