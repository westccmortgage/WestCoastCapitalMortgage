Who we are

Company overview

West Coast Capital Mortgage is a modern mortgage company focused on helping people buy homes, refinance, and build equity with confidence. We pair efficient technology with experienced, licensed guidance.

Our mission is simple: make financing clear, honest, and built around your goals.

  • Clear guidanceStraight answers and transparent options.
  • Smart solutionsA full range of programs for every borrower.
  • Technology + peopleModern tools backed by real advisors.
  • Borrower-firstNo hype, no pressure — just a smooth process.
Real people behind the guidance

Anatoliy Kanevsky

Founder • California Real Estate Broker

Anatoliy Kanevsky is the founder of West Coast Capital Mortgage and a California real estate and mortgage professional with more than 20 years of experience helping borrowers, homeowners, Realtors, investors, and self-employed clients navigate real-world financing scenarios.

His background combines mortgage lending, real estate brokerage, luxury residential development, and practical deal analysis. That experience shapes a company built around clear guidance, disciplined loan strategy, and real human review.
