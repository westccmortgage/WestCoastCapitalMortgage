MIAMI & SOUTH FLORIDA

DSCR financing for investors, including eligible foreign-national scenarios.

Miami investment financing often combines two questions: can the property support the loan, and can the borrower qualify without traditional US income documentation? We review DSCR, foreign-national, Non-QM, condo and investor scenarios together so the financing path is evaluated before the transaction gets too far.

WHY THIS QUERY MATTERS

Miami investors often need more than one financing category.

An international investor may have strong assets and a viable rental property but limited US credit or no conventional US tax-return history. A domestic investor may have complex income or multiple financed properties. The right review starts with the property, the expected rental economics, the borrower's residency and documentation profile, and the building itself if the property is a condo.

DSCR

Property cash flow

DSCR programs focus primarily on the relationship between qualifying rental income and the property's housing payment. Exact calculations and thresholds vary by lender.

FOREIGN NATIONAL

International-buyer documentation

Some programs may work with eligible non-US residents using passports, foreign banking or income documentation, reserves and other alternative documentation instead of a conventional US borrower profile.

NON-QM

Complex investor scenarios

Non-QM and portfolio lenders may offer alternatives when agency guidelines do not fit the borrower, occupancy, income documentation or property type.

WHAT TO SEND FIRST

A useful first review does not require a full loan package.

Property address or target area

If you already have a property, send the address. If not, send the city or neighborhood and expected purchase range.

Purchase price and expected rent

Estimated purchase price, down payment and market or lease rent help identify whether a DSCR review is appropriate.

Residency profile

Tell us whether the borrower is a US citizen, resident, visa holder or foreign national living abroad so the documentation path can be evaluated correctly.

Condo or single-family

Condo projects may require separate building-level review in addition to the borrower and property cash-flow analysis.

Intended rental use

Long-term rental, seasonal use and permitted short-term-rental strategies may be treated differently by lenders and local rules.

Liquidity and reserves

Available funds for down payment, closing costs and reserves can materially affect which lender programs are realistic.

FAQ

Miami DSCR and foreign-national financing questions.

Can a foreign national use DSCR financing in Miami?

Some lender programs may permit eligible foreign-national investors to qualify an investment property using a DSCR-based approach. Terms and documentation vary by program.

Do DSCR loans require US tax returns?

DSCR programs are designed around property cash flow rather than standard personal-income qualification, but lender documentation requirements still vary.

Can a Miami condo qualify?

Potentially. The borrower and DSCR may fit while the condo project still requires separate review for insurance, reserves, litigation, rental characteristics and other lender guidelines.

Can I close in an LLC?

Some investor programs may allow eligible entity vesting. The lender, title structure, guaranty requirements and legal or tax considerations should all be reviewed before closing.
