Start with the financing problem

California mortgage options by borrower scenario

City and home price are only part of a mortgage review. The requested loan amount, how income is documented, whether you want to use existing home equity, and an earlier denial can each change which financing path is worth considering. Choose the situation closest to yours.

Jumbo and high-balance purchase or refinance

Compare the requested loan amount with the applicable county conforming limit before selecting a program. Down payment, reserves, property type and lender requirements also matter.

Los Angeles County jumbo guide · Jumbo loan options

Self-employed and bank-statement borrowers

When tax-return income does not reflect business cash flow, review traditional qualification alongside possible bank-statement or other Non-QM documentation paths, subject to lender guidelines.

Self-employed mortgage review · Bank-statement loans

HELOC, home-equity loan or cash-out refinance

California homeowners who need to use existing equity can compare a HELOC, a fixed home-equity loan and cash-out refinancing. Review the impact on your current first mortgage, payments and closing costs before choosing a path. Eligibility depends on lender guidelines.

California HELOC and home-equity options · Cash-out refinance review

Mortgage denied or closing delayed?

If a lender declined a California purchase or refinance, identify the actual issue first: income calculation, reserves, appraisal, property or condo eligibility, or another underwriting condition. A second opinion is a review, not a promise of approval.
