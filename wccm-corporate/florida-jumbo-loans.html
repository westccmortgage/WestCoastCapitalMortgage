Direct answer

What is the 2026 jumbo threshold in Florida?

Most Florida counties: the 2026 one-unit conforming loan limit is $832,750. When the loan amount is above the applicable county conforming limit, the mortgage generally moves into jumbo financing.

Monroe County / Florida Keys: FHFA lists a higher 2026 one-unit conforming limit of $990,150. That means a loan amount that is jumbo in Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach, Orange or many other Florida counties may still be within the conforming ceiling in Monroe County.

These are Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac conforming loan limits, not a promise of approval or a lender-specific maximum. Property type, occupancy, loan-to-value, credit, reserves, income documentation and lender guidelines still matter.

The calculation that matters

Jumbo is based on the loan amount, not just the home price.

A higher-priced Florida property is not automatically a jumbo loan. The practical first calculation is purchase price minus down payment, then compare the resulting loan amount with the county limit.

Example: a $1,000,000 one-unit purchase in Palm Beach County with 20% down creates an $800,000 loan amount. That is below the 2026 Palm Beach County one-unit conforming limit of $832,750, although the file still must meet the selected program's eligibility requirements.

A $1,100,000 purchase with 20% down creates an $880,000 loan amount. In Palm Beach County that is above the 2026 conforming ceiling; in Monroe County it remains below the $990,150 one-unit county limit. The county can therefore change the financing category.

FAQ

Florida jumbo loan questions

What is the 2026 jumbo loan threshold in Florida?

For a one-unit property, most Florida counties use the $832,750 baseline conforming limit in 2026. Loan amounts above the applicable county limit generally move into jumbo financing. Monroe County has a higher one-unit limit of $990,150.

Is Monroe County different from Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach?

Yes. FHFA's 2026 county table lists Monroe County at $990,150 for a one-unit property, while Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties use the $832,750 baseline.

Does a $1 million Florida home automatically require a jumbo loan?

No. The loan amount after the down payment is the relevant comparison with the county conforming limit. A $1 million purchase with enough down payment may produce a loan amount below the applicable county threshold.

Can a self-employed borrower get a Florida jumbo loan?

Potentially. Jumbo programs vary. Some files use traditional income documentation; eligible borrowers may also have bank-statement or other Non-QM paths. Documentation, reserves, credit, property and underwriting requirements vary by lender and program.
