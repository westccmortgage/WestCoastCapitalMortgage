FAQ

Boca Raton mortgage questions.

Can a self-employed borrower qualify using bank statements?

Potentially. Some lenders offer bank-statement or other alternative-documentation programs for eligible self-employed borrowers. The qualifying calculation varies by lender and account type.

Can a Boca Raton investor qualify using rental cash flow?

Potentially. DSCR programs are designed for eligible investment properties and focus heavily on qualifying rental income relative to the property's housing payment.

Can a foreign national buy a Boca Raton property with financing?

Some programs are designed for eligible non-US residents. Documentation, down payment, reserves, property use and underwriting requirements vary by lender.

Does a condo require a different mortgage review?

Often yes. In addition to borrower underwriting, the lender may need to review the condominium project, insurance, association finances, reserves, litigation, repairs and rental restrictions.
