One deal, usually two loans

A short-term loan to buy and fix or build, then a sale or a long-term loan to keep it. We can do both, and our sister brokerage can help on the buying and selling side.

  1. BuyBring your own deal or find one with West Coast Capital Realty.
  2. Fix or buildUse a fix and flip or ground-up loan. The budget comes out in draws as the work gets done.

  4. Keep itOnce it’s rented, refinance into a DSCR or portfolio loan.

Questions investors ask us

What investor loans do you offer?
Fix and flip, ground-up construction, bridge, DSCR, rental portfolio and multifamily bridge loans for investors in California, Florida and Texas, closed in your LLC or corporation.
How much can I borrow?
Up to 90% of the price plus 100% of the rehab on a fix and flip, 75% of the land plus 100% of construction on a new build, up to 80% LTV on rentals, and $2M to $5M on apartment buildings.
Will you finance my first flip?
Yes. First-time investors can get up to 90% of the price plus 100% of the rehab, depending on credit and the project.
Do you verify my personal income?
No. Rental loans qualify on the rent, and short-term loans are sized on the property, your credit and your experience.
How do I get the rehab or construction money?
The budget is held back at closing and released in draws as the work is done: you request a draw online, an inspection confirms the progress and the funds go out.
Is there a prepayment penalty?
Not on fix and flip, construction or bridge loans. On rental loans you choose, including no penalty.
Can I keep the property as a rental when the work is done?
Yes. Once it is rented, refinance into a 30-year DSCR or portfolio loan, qualified on the rent.
Are these hard money loans?
They do the same job: short-term, interest-only loans based on the property and the deal. More on hard money loans in California.
Will checking my terms affect my credit score?
No. It is a soft pull that does not affect your score.
Which states do you lend in?
California, Florida and Texas. For investment property in other states, see West Coast Capital Investment, our investor-lending site, where you can check availability for your property. Terms can vary by market, and we confirm what applies to your property on the first call.
