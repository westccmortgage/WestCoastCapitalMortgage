What changed

Early lender limit vs. official FHFA limit

The new $845,000 figure is a lender program development, not a new FHFA limit. That distinction matters when comparing conforming, high-balance and jumbo options.

ReferenceOne-unit amountStatus
Official 2026 national baseline$832,750FHFA official
Early lender conforming limitUp to $845,000Select lenders; guidelines vary
Difference vs. 2026 baseline$12,250Potential additional conforming room where applicable

Important: an $840,000 loan is not automatically jumbo in California. High-cost counties can already have official 2026 conforming ceilings above the national baseline. The property county and unit count must be checked before choosing a loan path.

$840,000 loan

High-cost county

Check the county first

In a California high-cost county, the existing official county limit may already be above $845,000. In that case the early baseline change may not alter the conforming classification.
