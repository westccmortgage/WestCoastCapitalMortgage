How does a Florida bank statement mortgage work?

A bank-statement mortgage is an alternative income-documentation path that can be useful when a self-employed borrower's taxable net income does not fully reflect business cash flow. Participating lenders may analyze eligible deposits over a recent statement period instead of relying only on W-2 or tax-return net income.

West Coast Capital Mortgage reviews bank-statement scenarios for eligible borrowers and properties in Florida. Statement period, deposit treatment, business-expense analysis, credit, down payment, reserves, occupancy and property requirements vary by lender and file.

  • 1099 and independent contractorsIncome may require a different documentation approach.

FAQ

Florida bank statement mortgage questions

Are bank statement mortgage loans available for self-employed borrowers in Florida?
West Coast Capital Mortgage reviews bank-statement scenarios for eligible Florida borrowers and properties. Program availability and underwriting requirements vary by lender and file.
Can business bank statements be used?
Many programs can review business statements. The lender may apply an expense factor or another documented method to determine usable income.
What if another lender declined my mortgage?
A decline does not automatically mean another program will work, but a second review can identify whether income calculation, property eligibility, credit, reserves, documentation or program fit was the issue.
