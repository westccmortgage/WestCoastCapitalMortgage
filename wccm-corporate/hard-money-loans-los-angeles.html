Permits and draws

Rehab and construction money is held back at closing and released in draws after an inspection confirms the work. Permits come from the city the property is in: LADBS for the City of Los Angeles, including most of the Valley, or the building departments of Burbank, Glendale, Pasadena and the other cities.

On a new build, the land is financed at the highest leverage once permits are approved.

What happens after you send a deal

Same person from the first call to the payoff.

  1. We look at the numbersAddress, price, budget, what it will sell or rent for, and your track record. By form or by phone. No application and no credit check yet.
  2. You get a straight answerWhich program fits and roughly how much leverage to expect, before you pay for an appraisal. If the deal doesn’t work, we say so.
  3. Written termsCredit is checked with a soft pull that doesn’t touch your score. Then we order the appraisal and line up title, insurance and your entity documents.
  4. Close in your LLCThe rehab or construction budget is released through draws you request online as work is completed.

Questions about hard money in Los Angeles

Who offers hard money loans in Burbank and the San Fernando Valley?
West Coast Capital Mortgage, 150 E Olive Ave, Unit 112, Burbank. We offer fix and flip, bridge and construction loans across the Valley and the rest of Los Angeles County.
Do you lend in the City of Los Angeles, Glendale and Pasadena?
Yes, across Los Angeles County. Terms can vary by neighborhood, and we confirm them on the first call.
How much can I borrow on a Los Angeles flip?
Up to 90% of the price plus 100% of the rehab, within 70–75% of the after-repair value, from $50,000 to $3 million.
Can I get a loan for my first flip?
Yes. First-time investors can get up to 90% of the price plus 100% of a light or moderate rehab, depending on credit.
Can I build a new home on a lot or a teardown?
Yes, with a ground-up construction loan: up to 75% of the land with approved permits plus 100% of the construction budget, for builders with completed projects.
My flip is finished. Can I get a bridge loan while it sells?
Yes. A bridge loan covers up to 70% of value on a finished home that is listed for sale or leasing up.
Can I meet you in person?
Yes. The office is at 150 E Olive Ave, Unit 112, Burbank, CA 91502. Call 310-654-1577 to set a time.
Will checking my terms affect my credit score?
No. It is a soft pull that does not affect your score.
Do I need an LLC or corporation?
Yes, the loan closes in your LLC or corporation. No entity yet? Set one up before closing.
How fast can you close?
As fast as the appraisal and title allow. We order both right after the pre-screen.
