Why it matters

A mortgage is hard enough in your second language.

Underwriting conditions, rate locks and disclosures are written for people who grew up with American credit. When you can ask every question in your own language, you catch the problem before closing, not after.

As a broker, Anatoliy sends one file to several wholesale and private lenders and compares the terms. You talk to one person from the first call to closing.

FAQ

Questions

Can I go through the whole mortgage in Russian or Ukrainian?

Yes for the conversation: the application, the document list and every question can be handled in Russian or Ukrainian. The loan documents themselves are in English, and Anatoliy goes through each one with you in your language before you sign.

Можно ли оформить ипотеку полностью на русском?

Общение, анкета, список документов и все вопросы можно вести на русском или украинском. Сами кредитные документы на английском, и Анатолий проходит каждый из них с вами на русском до подписания.

I'm new to the U.S. and don't have a U.S. credit score. Is there a loan for me?

Possibly. Foreign-national and ITIN programs can work without a U.S. credit score, usually with a larger down payment. Whether your file fits depends on the lender's rules, the property and your documents.
