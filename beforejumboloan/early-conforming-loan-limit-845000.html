Up to $845,000

Before You Accept a Jumbo Path, Check the New Early Limit

Select lenders are offering early conforming loan limits up to $845,000. The official 2026 baseline is still $832,750, and high-cost county limits may already be higher than both.

Three lines now matter

Line1-unit amountUse
Official 2026 national baseline$832,750FHFA official baseline for most counties
Early lender limitUp to $845,000Select lenders only; availability and guidelines vary
Official county high-balance limitVaries by countyCan be substantially above $845,000 in high-cost markets
Official 2027 FHFA limitPendingNot announced as of Sept. 12, 2026

Example: $840,000 first mortgage

Baseline county: $840,000 is above the official 2026 baseline of $832,750, but it may fit a select lender's early $845,000 conforming program.

High-cost county: the same $840,000 may already be under the official county conforming limit, so it may never have needed a jumbo path in the first place.

What should change in our loan-limit tables?

The official FHFA county table should not be overwritten with $845,000. The county table remains the legal/agency reference. The early lender amount belongs beside it as a separate lender-program reference. That keeps the Strategy Studio accurate while still surfacing a timely financing opportunity.

Why this matters before jumbo

A borrower close to the line can sometimes compare three paths: the official county conforming limit, an available early lender conforming limit, and jumbo. Pricing, down payment, reserves, property type, occupancy and lender overlays can make one structure better than another. The point is to compare before assuming the loan must be jumbo.

Run the property county first

The Strategy Studio continues to use official 2026 FHFA county limits. After the official county line is established, ask whether an eligible early $845,000 lender option creates another path.
