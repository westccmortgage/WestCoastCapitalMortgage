2026 California Loan Limit Checker: Before assuming a purchase needs jumbo financing, check the property county and proposed loan amount. Free educational tool: beforejumboloan.com/california-jumbo-loan-limits-2026

Why this is useful before an offer

California buyers near a county's conforming ceiling may not know whether the proposed first mortgage falls into conforming, high-balance or jumbo review. That distinction can affect documentation, reserves, pricing and the financing conversation. The tool does not issue an approval; it gives the buyer and agent a clearer question to bring to the mortgage professional.

Seller needs to become the next buyer?

For a move-up buyer whose down payment or buying power is tied up in the current home, review the equity and timing before writing a sale-contingent offer. The California Buy-Before-You-Sell review collects both-property values, current debt and timing so the financing conversation can happen before the offer clock starts.
