Foreign National Loans for International Buyers

Buying US property from abroad? International buyers can often finance real estate in California and Florida through foreign national loan programs — frequently without a US credit score or Social Security Number. And an expensive home doesn’t automatically mean jumbo — check the structure first.

Can international buyers finance US property?

Yes. Many international buyers purchase second homes and investment property in the United States — California and Florida are two of the most popular destinations. You do not always need to pay all cash. Foreign national loan programs are designed for buyers who are not US citizens and may not have US credit, a Social Security Number, or US-based income.

These programs use alternative documentation — your passport, income and bank references from your home country, and reserves. And because a high price does not automatically mean a jumbo loan, it is worth checking the structure — county line, loan amount, and program path — before assuming the most expensive route.

FAQ

Foreign national loans — common questions

Can a foreign national get a mortgage in the United States?

Yes. Foreign national loan programs are designed for non-US citizens and non-residents who want to buy property in the US. Eligibility, terms, and documentation vary by lender and are subject to underwriting review.

Do I need a US credit score or Social Security Number?

Often no. Many foreign national programs use a passport, foreign credit references, and international bank statements instead of a US credit score or SSN. Requirements vary by lender.

How much down payment do foreign national buyers usually need?

Foreign national loans typically require a larger down payment than standard mortgages. The amount depends on the lender, property type, and borrower profile, and is subject to program guidelines.

Is a foreign national loan always a jumbo loan?

Not necessarily. The loan amount versus the county conforming limit determines whether a loan is jumbo. A foreign national loan can be structured at different loan sizes — checking the structure before assuming jumbo is the idea behind Before Jumbo Loan.

What documents are usually needed?

Commonly a valid passport (and visa if applicable), proof of income from your home country, international bank statements and reserves, and sometimes a reference letter from your foreign bank. Requirements vary by lender.
