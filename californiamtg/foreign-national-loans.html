Foreign National Loans for International Buyers in California

Buying a home or investment property in California from abroad? International buyers can often finance US real estate through foreign national loan programs — frequently without a US credit score or Social Security Number.

Can international buyers finance property in California?

Yes. California is one of the most sought-after destinations in the world for international buyers — from Los Angeles and Orange County to the Bay Area and coastal communities. You do not always need to pay all cash. Foreign national loan programs are designed for buyers who are not US citizens and may not have US credit, a Social Security Number, or US-based income.

These programs use alternative documentation — your passport, income and bank references from your home country, and reserves — so qualified international buyers can finance a California purchase instead of tying up all of their capital.

Foreign national loans — common questions

Can a foreign national buy property in California with a mortgage?

Yes. Foreign national loan programs are designed for non-US citizens and non-residents who want to buy property in California. Eligibility and terms vary by lender and are subject to underwriting review.

Do international buyers need a US credit score or SSN?

Often no. Many foreign national programs use a passport, foreign credit references, and international bank statements instead of a US credit score or Social Security Number. Requirements vary by lender.

How much down payment do foreign national buyers usually need?

Foreign national loans typically require a larger down payment than standard mortgages. The amount depends on the lender, property type, and borrower profile, and is subject to program guidelines.

Can I buy a California investment or second home as a foreign national?

Yes. Many international buyers purchase second homes or investment property in California. Depending on the scenario, foreign national or DSCR programs may fit.

What documents are usually needed?

Commonly a valid passport (and visa if applicable), proof of income from your home country, international bank statements and reserves, and sometimes a reference letter from your foreign bank. Requirements vary by lender.
