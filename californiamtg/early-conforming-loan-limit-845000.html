2027 Conforming Loan Limit: $855,400

On November 24, 2026, FHFA announced the 2027 conforming loan limits. The one-unit baseline rises to $855,400 from $832,750, and the high-cost ceiling is $1,283,100. In California the limit that applies depends on the property’s county.

The numbers at a glance

One-unit limitAmountWhat it means
2026 baseline$832,750Applies through 2026
2027 baseline$855,400Up $22,650; most U.S. counties
2027 high-cost ceiling$1,283,100150% of the baseline; the highest limit in the contiguous U.S.
Early lender limits (September 2026)$845,000–$850,000Were offered by some lenders before FHFA’s announcement; the official 2027 limit is higher

What changes for California borrowers

In a county at the baseline, a one-unit loan up to $855,400 can be conforming under the 2027 limits. A loan of $850,000 that was above the 2026 baseline may no longer need a jumbo program.

Check the county first

Many California counties have their own limit above the baseline, up to the $1,283,100 ceiling. The comparison that matters is the property’s county, the number of units and the loan amount. FHFA publishes every county’s limit.
