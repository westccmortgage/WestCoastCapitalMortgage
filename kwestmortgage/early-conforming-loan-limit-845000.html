Does the New Early $845,000 Limit Change Key West Financing?

Usually not the county line itself. Monroe County is already a high-cost area with an official 2026 one-unit conforming limit of $990,150.

Key West numbers at a glance

Reference1-unit amountKey West relevance
Official 2026 national baseline$832,750National baseline
Early lender baselineUp to $845,000Select lenders; not an FHFA county-limit replacement
Official 2026 Monroe County limit$990,150Current official one-unit county line for Key West / Monroe County
Official 2027 FHFA limitPendingNot announced as of Sept. 12, 2026
Why we are not changing the Strategy Console to $845,000: the console should continue using the official Monroe County line of $990,150 for a one-unit property in 2026. The $845,000 figure is an early lender baseline program, not a new FHFA Monroe County limit.

What this means for a $900,000 Key West loan

A one-unit $900,000 loan is above the national 2026 baseline and above the early $845,000 baseline, but it is still below Monroe County's official 2026 conforming limit of $990,150. That is exactly why Key West borrowers should check the county line before assuming they need jumbo financing.

What this means for a Florida property outside Monroe County

Other Florida counties may use the national baseline or another county-specific limit. If the applicable official 2026 line is $832,750, an eligible early lender limit up to $845,000 could matter for a loan near that range.
